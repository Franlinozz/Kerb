## Safe core claims

Kerb is the market-time risk layer for tokenized stocks on X Layer.

Kerb measures executable depth in real X Layer pools.

The current depth path uses a Uniswap V3 tick-walk with an OKX DEX cross-check where available.

Kerb publishes market-time credit terms.

Carry and Session Max have different survival horizons.

The liquidation threshold is fixed and does not move with session state.

A Session Max position can become curable at Last Call.

A Cure repays only the required difference toward Carry, not necessarily the whole loan.

Kerb Credit is on X Layer testnet with mirror collateral and mUSDG.

The risk plane is on X Layer mainnet.

The contracts are unaudited.

Kerb Terms are reproducible from published inputs.

KerbQuote and agent endpoints are additional consumers of Kerb Terms.
