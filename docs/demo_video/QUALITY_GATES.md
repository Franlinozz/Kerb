# Quality Gates

## Gate A: Comprehension

After one viewing, a judge should be able to answer:

- What problem does Kerb solve?
- What is C(1%)?
- Why are Carry and Session Max different?
- What is Last Call?
- What does a Cure do?
- Why does the liquidation threshold stay fixed?
- What runs on mainnet?
- What runs on testnet?
- Why is Kerb more than Kerb Credit?
- How can a term be verified?

If three or more answers are unclear, rewrite the edit.

## Gate B: Hero moment

The Last Call and Cure sequence must show, in order:
- a Session Max position exists
- Last Call opens
- a curable obligation appears
- another wallet can act
- the Cure confirms
- the resulting state is visibly safer or no longer requires the same Cure

If this sequence is confusing, the film fails regardless of polish.

## Gate C: Product truth

No unsupported claim.

No hidden testnet distinction.

No fake number.

No altered UI text.

No fake transaction.

No AI-generated product substitute.

## Gate D: Visual quality

Check:
- readable at 100 percent playback in a normal browser
- no 4K canvas with tiny 1080p UI
- no stretched recording
- no low-resolution crop held too long
- no cursor parked over important text
- no notification popups
- no desktop clutter
- no black frames between cuts
- no inconsistent gamma
- no blown highlights
- no excessive sharpening
- no mismatched frame rate judder

## Gate E: Motion quality

Motion should:
- direct attention
- explain state
- compress time
- reinforce brand structure

Motion should not:
- decorate empty space
- repeat on every cut
- use the same whoosh repeatedly
- move faster than the viewer can read

## Gate F: Audio quality

Listen on:
- headphones
- laptop speakers

Check:
- every word intelligible
- acronyms pronounced correctly
- no music masking
- no sudden loudness changes
- no clipping
- no obvious TTS splice
- no awkward breath or pause around scene cuts

## Gate G: Runtime

Hard:
2:00 minimum.
4:00 maximum.

Preferred:
3:05 to 3:30.

Target:
about 3:18.

## Gate H: Three-mode review

Watch once:
- normally
- muted
- audio only

Muted:
the product story still works.

Audio only:
the narration still describes a coherent system and never depends on an unseen unsupported number.

## Gate I: First 30 seconds

The first 30 seconds must contain:
- the problem
- Kerb's category
- real product footage
- live market state or executable capacity

No long logo intro.

## Gate J: Final 20 seconds

The ending must contain:
- honest mainnet/testnet distinction
- no new feature
- one final thesis
- one clean visual close

Final spoken sentence:
`Never lend more than you can liquidate.`
